## Findings

- **[P1] Stepper dots are 3 px tall touch targets.** `.dot` in style.css, built in app.js stepper/derivation.
  Impact: on a phone the step dots cannot be tapped reliably. Fix: keep the 3 px bar visually, give the
  button 24 px of hit area (padding plus background-clip). Command: adapt.
- **[P2] Heading order skips h2 inside lessons.** app.js `blockTitle`, summary, terms, goals use h3 under the
  page h1. Impact: screen reader outline is broken. Fix: h2 for block titles. Command: harden.
- **[P2] Reduced motion is a global 0.01 ms kill.** style.css `@media (prefers-reduced-motion)`. Impact:
  state feedback (answer reveal, seam flash) disappears rather than becoming a fade. Fix: keep opacity
  fades, drop transforms only. Command: animate.
- **[P2] Small touch targets on phone.** Breadcrumb links 15 px tall, copy button 48x22, range inputs 22 px.
  Fix: min-height 44 px hit areas at narrow widths. Command: adapt.
- **[P3] Editor line numbers 3.1:1 (light) / 3.5:1 (dark).** `.ed-gutter`. Fix: lighten to 60%. Command: polish.

## Positive findings

- Contrast of all body and label text passes AA in both themes.
- Every interactive element has an accessible name; skip link, focus ring in patina.
- Chapter data is loaded per chapter, so the first paint does not parse 11 MB.
